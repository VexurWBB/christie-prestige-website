Growing up, Liam watched home ownership become increasingly difficult for many in his generation and became fascinated by what creates long-term value and helps ordinary Australians build wealth through property.

Long before property, Liam learned the value of discipline as a competitive gymnast and trampoline athlete, representing Australia at the 2016 Youth Olympic Games. Years of training taught him resilience, preparation and the importance of staying focused on long-term goals—qualities that now shape the way he approaches every client engagement.

Liam purchased his own home before expanding into property investment, giving him firsthand experience of the excitement, uncertainty and responsibility that comes with ownership. When an investment property suffered significant tenant damage, he experienced at real cost the importance of careful due diligence and surrounding yourself with trusted professionals.

“ Buying well is only the beginning; success depends upon having the right people beside you long after settlement.

Before entering the property profession, Liam worked as an airport baggage handler before progressing into a training manager role. Those years taught humility, work ethic and respect for every dollar earned. Recently married and building a future with his wife, he understands the realities of mortgages, budgets and long-term financial planning because he is living them himself.

Working alongside his father brings together two complementary perspectives: Ben's decades of leadership and decision-making experience, and Liam's lived understanding of the challenges facing today's buyers. They are united by one philosophy—the Christie Standard—and a shared commitment to helping clients make thoughtful property decisions with confidence, clarity and integrity.

Liam's mission is simple: to help Australians buy the right property with confidence, supported by honest advice, disciplined research and genuine advocacy every step of the way.
