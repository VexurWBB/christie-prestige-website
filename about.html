Ben Christie

Christie Prestige Property did not begin with property. It began with responsibility.

BC

Ben Christie

  • 01 Military aviation & leadership
  • 02 Operational command, RAAF
  • 03 Executive coaching

For more than two decades, Ben Christie served as a military pilot and senior leader in the Royal Australian Air Force, where important decisions carried real consequences. Aviation taught him that the quality of an outcome rarely depends upon luck or perfect information—it depends upon preparation, disciplined judgement, clarity under pressure and the integrity to continually reassess reality as it evolves.

Throughout a career spanning operational command, international appointments, executive leadership and organisational coaching, Ben discovered that the same principles which keep aircraft safely on course also underpin wise decisions in life.

Clear objectives. Thoughtful preparation. Discernment amidst complexity. And the humility to adjust course when circumstances change—lessons that now shape Christie Prestige Property.

“ It deserves more than salesmanship. It deserves stewardship.

Ben believes purchasing a home or investment property is one of the most significant decisions a family will ever make. Rather than promising certainty or chasing the latest market hype, he helps clients understand complexity, weigh competing priorities and make decisions they will still be proud of years into the future.

Whether assisting a first-home buyer, a medical professional, a Defence family, an expatriate returning home or an experienced investor, his objective remains constant: to improve the quality of important property decisions while building trusted relationships that endure well beyond settlement.

For Ben, property has never been the destination. Helping people build secure futures through thoughtful decisions is.

Buyer's Agent

Property represents freedom, security and opportunity—not just bricks and mortar.

LC

Liam Christie

  • 01 Youth Olympic gymnast
  • 02 Homeowner & investor
  • 03 Next-generation advocacy

Growing up, Liam watched home ownership become increasingly difficult for many in his generation and became fascinated by what creates long-term value and helps ordinary Australians build wealth through property.

Long before property, Liam learned discipline as a competitive gymnast and trampoline athlete, representing Australia at the 2016 Youth Olympic Games—building resilience, preparation and focus on long-term goals.

He purchased his own home before expanding into investment, learning firsthand the excitement, uncertainty and responsibility of ownership—including the real cost of poor due diligence.

“ Honest advice, disciplined research and genuine advocacy every step of the way.

Before entering the property profession, Liam worked as an airport baggage handler before progressing into a training manager role—years that taught humility, work ethic and respect for every dollar earned. Recently married and building a future with his wife, he understands the realities of mortgages, budgets and long-term financial planning because he is living them himself.

Working alongside his father brings together two complementary perspectives: Ben's decades of leadership and Liam's lived understanding of the challenges facing today's buyers.

Liam's mission is to help Australians buy the right property with confidence—supported by honest advice, disciplined research and genuine advocacy every step of the way.
