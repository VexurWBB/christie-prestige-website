Founder & Buyer's Agent

Ben Christie

Christie Prestige Property did not begin with property. It began with responsibility.

For more than two decades, Ben Christie served as a military pilot and senior leader in the Royal Australian Air Force, where important decisions carried real consequences. Aviation taught him that the quality of an outcome rarely depends upon luck or perfect information. It depends upon preparation, disciplined judgement, clarity under pressure and the integrity to continually reassess reality as it evolves.

Throughout a career spanning operational command, international appointments, executive leadership and organisational coaching, Ben discovered that the same principles which keep aircraft safely on course also underpin wise decisions in life. Clear objectives. Thoughtful preparation. Discernment amidst complexity. And the humility to adjust course when circumstances change.

Those lessons now shape Christie Prestige Property. Ben believes purchasing a home or investment property is one of the most significant decisions a family will ever make. It deserves more than salesmanship. It deserves stewardship.

Rather than promising certainty or chasing the latest market hype, Ben helps clients understand complexity, weigh competing priorities and make decisions they will still be proud of years into the future. His philosophy—The Christie Standard—was gradually uncovered through decades of leadership, service, parenting, learning and lived experience. It is built upon clarity, discernment, stewardship and integrity.

Whether assisting a first-home buyer, a medical professional, a Defence family, an expatriate returning home or an experienced investor, Ben's objective remains constant: to improve the quality of important property decisions while building trusted relationships that endure well beyond settlement.

For Ben, property has never been the destination. Helping people build secure futures through thoughtful decisions is.
